A five-step walkthrough

From first curve to printable model.

You don't need to begin with a blank 3D mesh. The studio guides the design from a simple profile to a model you can prepare in your slicer.

Open the Design Studio

Launch the browser-based workspace. The 3D view and parameter panel are where you preview and adjust your lamp design.

Shape your lamp

Start from a profile option, then edit its Bézier curve and radius endpoints to create the outline you want.

Choose a surface pattern

Apply sinusoidal ribs, origami knurling, pleated fins, a smooth finish, or cylindrical Voronoi cutouts.

Customize and preview

Adjust dimensions, shell and pattern parameters, and available socket fitting options. Rotate the 3D preview to inspect the result.

Export for your printing workflow

Export the model as a binary STL, then open it in a compatible slicer such as Cura, PrusaSlicer, or Bambu Studio to review printer-specific settings.
