Crafting Beautiful Light Through Mathematical Geometry

LuminaPrint 3D eliminates the barrier between complex architectural CAD software and desktop 3D printing. Traditional modeling of organic Voronoi lattices, twisted fluting, and dual-wall mounting spiders can take hours of manual lofting and boolean operations.

With our in-browser parametric studio, every radius, rib wave, helical twist, and cellular cutout updates instantly in real time. Download a guaranteed watertight binary .STL file, drop it directly into your slicer, and print without support material.

Manufacturing Best Practices

3D Slicer Recommendations for Optimal Illumination

Follow these proven settings in PrusaSlicer, Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, or Cura for translucent diffusion and structural strength.

1. Material Selection

Use Translucent / Natural PLA or PETG for bright diffuse ambient lighting, or Matte White for soft architectural porcelain look.

• Translucent PLA • PETG
2. Perimeter & Infill

Set infill to 0%. Use 2–3 perimeters (0.8–1.2mm shell) for hollow shades to ensure light passes without infill shadows.

• 0% Infill • 2-3 Perimeters
3. Cool LED Bulbs

Always pair 3D-printed lampshades with modern Cool LED Bulbs (5W–9W). Avoid incandescent or halogen bulbs to prevent thermal warping.

• LED Only (Max 12W)
