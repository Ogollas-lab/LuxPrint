A study in shape and surface

Different forms. One design studio.

These visual studies illustrate the kinds of silhouettes and patterns you can explore. They are examples, not built-in presets.

Minimal ribbed shade

Repeating vertical ribs bring a soft cadence to a clean lamp silhouette.

Geometric lantern

Voronoi openings create a cellular surface that lets light pass through.

Pleated architectural

Evenly repeated fins produce a crisp, folded profile.

Origami surface

Angular, repeating facets give the surface a layered geometric character.

Contemporary cylinder

A restrained cylindrical form offers a simple starting point for customization.

Gallery artwork is illustrative. Create and customize your own geometry in the studio; these examples are not downloadable presets.
